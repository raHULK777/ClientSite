## 1. What is already true

- **Mechanism:** one deployment gives every contractor a host-resolved, fully branded workspace where a client can price a job and sign a change order without a phone call.
- **Scene:** a homeowner at 8pm on a phone, holding two contractor quotes, deciding who feels straight with them; the contractor's office manager wanting to look established without a web project.
- **Cultural home:** the paper and site world of residential construction — plan sets, permit stamps, material samples, trade catalogs, job signage, framing marks, survey stakes.
- **First surface must prove:** a real, itemized price can be produced right here, and the contractor behind it looks like a serious business.

### The ruts (kept out of the candidate list)

1. **What the category always ships:** full-bleed house photo, "Building your dream home" headline, three service cards, testimonial carousel, contact form.
2. **Its predictable opposite:** dark blueprint-grid landing, mono type, neon-orange CTA, "construction tech" dashboard screenshots.
3. **Nearby rut the brief invites:** navy + orange + cream contractor template. The pinned demo palette is tenant data, not a license to default to it.

### Physical object / pre-web

What it is as an object: a **job binder** — plan sheets, a carbon-copy estimate pad, permit paperwork, material chips, a stamped approval.
Before the web: plan sets on a tailgate, carbon-copy change orders, permit stamps, catalogs with index tabs.

## 2. Seven material systems, ordered by resonance

| # | World | Family | Why it carries the mechanism | How it looks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Plan set / drawing sheet** | Technical drawing | The authoritative document of the trade, and dimension lines make price transparency literal | Cool paper white, near-black ink, faint drafting grid, red revision marks, title block, sheet index |
| 2 | **Permit stamp / municipal form** | Bureaucratic paperwork | Approval is the product's core act; a stamp is legal finality | Off-white form stock, ruled boxes, dense small print, one ink color, typewriter labels, rubber stamp |

| 5 | **Job-site signage / safety marking** | Signage | The site is the product's stage; status must be legible at distance | Fluorescent safety yellow, black condensed caps, hard rectangles, stencil numerals, caution stripes |

## 3. The assigned direction

### THE JOB SET — the contractor's plan set for the client

Not a blueprint poster. A real plan sheet, working as a page: title block, sheet index, dimension lines, revision clouds, and a takeoff schedule that fills in as the visitor answers.

- **World:** cool paper white ground with a faint 1/8" drafting grid; near-black ink; tenant color as the title-block field and primary action; revision color (tenant secondary, or a functional red when the tenant palette cannot carry it) reserved for changes and totals. Type is a drafting grotesque with a width axis, not monospace-as-costume. Numbers are tabular; project and sheet numbers can use a stencil cut.
- **First viewport:** a full-height sheet. The left column is the drawing title and the plain-language offer; the right column is a live **takeoff sheet** with the first three scope choices already selectable. The primary action ("Price my project") is inside the sheet, not a link to a form. The title block runs along the bottom edge: contractor name, license, phone, service area.
- **Visitor path:** title → choose scope inline → dimensions → material tier → the takeoff sheet totals with itemized lines, a range, and assumptions in margin notes → request review. No login, no modal.
- **Signature interaction:** answering the estimator **drafts the quote**. Dimension lines extend as fields gain values; line items land in the schedule; a revision cloud draws around the total; a "READY TO REVIEW" stamp lands once. 300–500ms, once, reduced-motion path is a plain fade.
- **Cross-surface reach:** the app uses the sheet index as navigation, revision clouds as change-order status, and the schedule table as the project list; the field surface is the same sheet in "site copy" (higher contrast, larger controls); the client approval becomes the stamped receipt.
- **Honest risk:** drafting language can intimidate a homeowner, and the world collapses into the "blueprint tech" rut if it becomes blue grid + mono + neon. Plain-language microcopy, real material photography at the edges, and no blueprint-blue ground are the guardrails.

### Raised by the hand it beat (named donors)

- **Permit stamp → evidence floor:** every approval block shows signer, timestamp, IP, and scope in ruled boxes, stamped with finality. Trust is evidenced, not asserted.
- **Trade catalog → index discipline:** the estimator is a spec schedule with a real index and a comparison column, never four pricing cards.
- **Job signage → distance legibility:** project numbers and status marks use stencil-cut numerals, legible at arm's length on a phone in sun.
- **Framing layout → measured margins:** dimension ticks bracket totals and progress; precision is visible, not claimed.
- **Survey stake → grounding:** project phases sit on a site/contour timeline, not a generic stepper.

## 4. Verdicts

| Candidate | Verdict | Reason | Kept discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Permit stamp | **Competitive** (product clarity) | Approval finality is the product's core; weak on homeowner desire | Evidence floor |
| Trade catalog | **Competitive** (estimator clarity) | Density and comparison serve the quote; weak emotion | Index discipline |
| Material sample board | **Impeccable's pick** (audience identification) | The warmest, most homeowner-legible world; weakest at proving software | Material honesty (holds the assigned direction honest about texture) |
| Job signage | **Declined** | Legibility at distance is a discipline, not a page world; full-surface use becomes a safety-yellow costume | Distance legibility |
| Framing layout | **Declined** | Beautiful but too quiet to carry an offer; measurement is a device, not a world | Measured margins |
| Survey stake | **Declined** | Spatial phase storytelling is strong, but it cannot carry a working quote on the first viewport | Grounding |

### IMPECCABLE'S PICK — Material sample board

The strongest grounded direction that is not the assignment. Full-bleed material textures, labeled chips, a quote that assembles like a selection board. Honest risk: it is familiar and warm, and it can read as a design-build portfolio rather than software; the mechanism would have to be proven by a working quote on the board.

### Standing exit — the conventional contractor site

Hero photograph, "Building your dream home," three services, one testimonial, contact form — played straight at full craft, no irony. Always available; never recommended.
