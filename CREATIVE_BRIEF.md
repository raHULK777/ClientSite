## GEMINI IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS

If you provide your Gemini API key, here are ready-to-use prompts:

### Prompt 1: Hero Contractor Photo
```
Professional photography of a confident Hispanic male contractor in his 40s, wearing 
a high-vis vest over a clean work shirt, hard hat slightly tilted, holding a clipboard 
on a residential construction site. Warm golden hour lighting. The scene shows an 
active kitchen remodel in the background — exposed framing, tools organized, clean 
job site. Authentic, not staged-looking. Shot on 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. 
Style: commercial construction photography, trustworthy, professional.
```

### Prompt 2: Kitchen Remodel Reveal
```
Wide-angle interior shot of a beautifully remodeled modern kitchen. White shaker 
cabinets, quartz countertops, subway tile backsplash, stainless steel appliances. 
Natural light streaming through large windows. Clean, organized, aspirational but 
realistic — not a showroom. Warm wood accent wall. Style: architectural 
photography, Houzz magazine quality, inviting and trustworthy.
```

### Prompt 3: Phone Portal Mockup
```
A modern smartphone (iPhone style) held against a blurred construction site background. 
The screen shows a clean mobile app interface — a contractor project dashboard with 
a timeline showing "Scope agreed" and "Work in progress", a pending change order 
card with "$2,400" and an "Approve" button. UI is clean, professional, with orange 
and navy blue accent colors. The phone has a slight drop shadow. Style: product 
photography, tech mockup, clean and modern.
```

### Prompt 4: Before/After Ad Creative
```
Split-screen advertisement layout. LEFT side: a dim, outdated Google Business 
profile screenshot with no website, text overlay "Before: Invisible Online". 
RIGHT side: a vibrant, professional construction contractor website on a laptop 
with project photos, clear CTAs, text overlay "After: Professional & Booking 
Leads". Bold divider in the center with an arrow pointing right. Orange and 
navy blue color scheme. Style: marketing ad creative, high contrast, clear 
visual hierarchy, social media ad format.
```

### Prompt 5: Crew Photo
```
Three construction workers (diverse — one Black male, one Hispanic male, one 
White female) standing on a residential job site, wearing hard hats and safety 
vests, arms crossed or holding tools, confident and approachable expressions. 
Background shows a partially remodeled home interior. Natural daylight, 
authentic candid feel. Style: documentary construction photography, trustworth 
and professional, diverse team representation.
```

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

1. **All images should be web-optimized** — target <200KB per image for web use
2. **Hero images:** 1920x1080 minimum for desktop, 1080x1350 for mobile
3. **Social ads:** Generate both 1:1 (1080x1080) and 4:5 (1080x1350) versions
4. **Mockups:** Use realistic device frames (iPhone 15, MacBook Pro, iPad)
5. **Consistency:** Maintain the orange/navy color scheme across all generated assets
